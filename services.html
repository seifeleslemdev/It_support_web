Basic troubleshooting steps

  1. Write down the error message and when the problem started.
  2. Check power, cables, Wi-Fi, and other connections.
  3. Restart the affected device or application, if it is safe to do so.
  4. Collect your device and system information before requesting help.
  5. Submit a support ticket if the problem continues.

For more help, contact the support team.
